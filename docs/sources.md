# Real-world figures and sources

Figures used in the README, the Devpost write-up and the deck. Only figures confirmed by at least two outlets are used; where outlets differ, the conservative figure is given.

| Figure | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Online crime cases in Malaysia, Jan–Nov 2025 | **67,735** | PDRM statement, 8 Dec 2025, via [Bernama](https://bernama.com/misc/rss/news.php?id=2500014) and [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2025/12/08/malaysians-swindled-out-of-rm27b-in-cyber-scams-police-data-reveals/201156) |
| Losses in those cases | **over RM2.7 billion** | same |
| Largest category by cases: telecommunications scams (calls) | **28,698 cases**, RM715.7 million lost | same (Bukit Aman CCID breakdown) |
| Largest category by loss: non-existent investments | **over RM1.37 billion** | same |
| NSRC 997 complaint calls in 2025 | **about 146,000** | Home Minister, Mar 2026, via [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/03/03/call-997-right-away-if-you-suspect-a-scam-fahmi-tells-malaysians/211156) |
| Funds saved by NSRC in 2025 | **about RM34.5 million** | same (outlets give RM34.05–34.53 million) |
| Funds returned to victims via NSRC | RM508,407 (2024) → about RM6.7 million (2025) | same |

**What this says for CrowdShield:** money is recovered for only a small share of losses once it has left; the biggest lever is stopping the transfer before it happens (the payee check with the live call signal), and getting victims to 997 within minutes (the bot's call sheet). Calls are the most common scam channel, which is the signal only the crowd sees.

Live news in the app (landing page, "Scams in the Malaysian news") comes from a Tavily news search, filtered by Nemotron 3 Super, with every item linked to its source.
